About Scotty

Sports are fun.
Talking about them
should be too.

Scotty Gange is a Sports Anchor and Reporter at 9NEWS KUSA-TV in Denver, Colorado. A graduate of Arizona State University's Walter Cronkite School of Journalism, Scotty brings energy, warmth, and genuine enthusiasm to every broadcast.

In 2020, he was named the Jim Nantz Award winner - given to the nation's top collegiate sportscaster. He has since been recognized as Colorado Sportscaster of the Year four times, covering the Broncos, Rockies, Avalanche, and Colorado's prep sports landscape.

Off camera, he runs a Daily Positivity Newsletter that lands in inboxes every morning - because the same energy he brings to the desk, he brings to everything he does.
